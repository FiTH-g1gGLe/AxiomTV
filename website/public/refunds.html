Refund policy

Last updated 15 November 2026

Every device gets a 3-day free trial with every feature, so you can make sure AxiomTV works with your device and your provider before you pay.

After you've paid

Because you get the full app to try first, and your device is unlocked straight away when you pay, we don't give refunds if you simply change your mind. At checkout you agree to get access immediately, which means the usual 14-day cancellation period for online purchases doesn't apply.

If something's wrong

This doesn't affect your legal rights. If AxiomTV doesn't work as described on your device, message us and we'll try to fix it. If we can't, we'll refund you. We'll also refund you if you were charged twice by mistake.

How to ask

Message us on Discord or email support@axiomtv.co.uk with your device code (Settings → Licence, or on the payment screen) and your order number from your Lemon Squeezy receipt. Refunds go back to your original payment method through Lemon Squeezy. When a payment is refunded, the device it unlocked is locked again.
