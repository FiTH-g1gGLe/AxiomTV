Privacy policy

Last updated 17 November 2026

AxiomTV is a media player for Android devices. This page explains what information AxiomTV and this website use, and why. We keep it to the minimum needed to run the free trial and unlock devices.

What we collect from the app

  • A scrambled device identifier. The app sends your device's Android ID (or a random ID it creates). Our server only stores a one-way scrambled version of it, which can't be turned back into the original.
  • Your device code (for example 7K3P-9QXD), which our server creates for your device.
  • Basic device details: the device model and the AxiomTV version, plus when the app last checked in.
  • Licence status: whether your device is on trial, unlocked or locked, and any moves between devices.

What we collect when you buy

Payments are handled by Stripe, who act as the seller and merchant of record. We never see or store your card details. Stripe sends us your email address and order number so we can unlock your device, let you move your unlock to a new device, and handle refunds. Stripe's own privacy policy covers the payment itself.

What we don't collect

  • Your provider login details (server, username, password) stay on your device. They are never sent to us.
  • We don't record what channels or programmes you watch.
  • There are no adverts, no tracking and no analytics, and we never sell your information.

Other services the app uses

  • Your provider — the app connects directly to the provider you sign in with.
  • TMDB (The Movie Database) — for posters, artwork and cast. The app asks TMDB about the titles you browse.
  • GitHub — the app checks GitHub for new versions and downloads updates from there.

Where it's stored and for how long

Licence information is stored on Cloudflare's servers. We keep it for as long as your device is on trial or unlocked, so that your purchase keeps working. Ask us and we'll delete your details; note that a deleted unlock can't be restored.

Your rights

Under UK data protection law you can ask to see, correct or delete the information we hold about you. Contact us on Discord or at support@axiomtv.co.uk with your device code. If you're unhappy with how we've handled your information, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ico.org.uk).

This website

This website doesn't use cookies or analytics. Its fonts are loaded from Google Fonts.
