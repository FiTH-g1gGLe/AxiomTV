Install AxiomTV

Works on Android TV devices, Fire TV / Fire Stick and Android phones.

On a TV device or Fire Stick

  1. Install the free Downloader app from your device's app store.
  2. Allow Downloader to install apps (it will ask, or find it under Settings → Security / Developer options).
  3. Open Downloader and type this address:
    get.axiomtv.co.uk
  4. Install, open AxiomTV and sign in with your provider.

Download not starting? Use the backup address instead: download.axiomtv.co.uk

On an Android phone

  1. On your phone, tap Download AxiomTV (or the backup download).
  2. Open the downloaded file and allow your browser to install apps if asked.
  3. Open AxiomTV and sign in with your provider.

AxiomTV updates itself — you'll get a prompt when a new version is ready.

Questions

Does AxiomTV come with channels?

No. AxiomTV is a player only. You need your own subscription with a provider that gives you Xtream login details, and you're responsible for making sure your provider and what you watch are legal where you live.

Is it a subscription?

No. You pay once and that device is unlocked for life, including future updates.

How does the free trial work?

Every device gets 3 days of full use. When the trial ends, AxiomTV shows a QR code — scan it, pay on your phone, and the device unlocks by itself. No card is needed for the trial.

I've got a new device. Do I have to pay again?

No. On your old device go to Settings → Licence → Move to another device, then on the new device choose "Already paid?" and type the code. If your old device is lost or broken, use the email and order number from your receipt. You can move it yourself once every 30 days — message us if you need help.

Does one payment cover all my devices?

One payment unlocks one device. Each device or phone you want to use needs its own unlock.

Can I get a refund?

The free trial is there so you can try everything first, so we don't normally refund after you've paid. If AxiomTV doesn't work as described on your device, message us and we'll put it right or refund you. See the refund policy.

How do I get help?

Join our Discord (discord.axiomtv.co.uk) and post in the help forum. Have your device code ready: it's in Settings → Licence, or on the payment screen.

Need help or want to report a problem? Join the AxiomTV Discord at discord.axiomtv.co.uk and post in the help forum with your device code.
