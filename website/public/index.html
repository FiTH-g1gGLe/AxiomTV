Install AxiomTV

Works on Android TV boxes, Fire TV / Fire Stick and Android phones.

On a TV box or Fire Stick

  1. Install the free Downloader app from your box's app store.
  2. Allow Downloader to install apps (it will ask, or find it under Settings → Security / Developer options).
  3. Open Downloader and type this address:
    axiomtv-site.dj-giggle.workers.dev/get
  4. Install, open AxiomTV and sign in with your provider.

On an Android phone

  1. On your phone, tap Download AxiomTV.
  2. Open the downloaded file and allow your browser to install apps if asked.
  3. Open AxiomTV and sign in with your provider.

AxiomTV updates itself — you'll get a prompt when a new version is ready.

Questions

Does AxiomTV come with channels?

No. AxiomTV is a player only. You need your own subscription with a provider that gives you Xtream login details, and you're responsible for making sure your provider and what you watch are legal where you live.

Is it a subscription?

No. You pay once and that device is unlocked for life, including future updates.

How does the free trial work?

Every device gets 3 days of full use. When the trial ends, AxiomTV shows a QR code — scan it, pay on your phone, and the box unlocks by itself. No card is needed for the trial.

I've got a new box. Do I have to pay again?

No. On your old box go to Settings → Licence → Move to another box, then on the new box choose "Already paid?" and type the code. If your old box is lost or broken, use the email and order number from your receipt. You can move it yourself once every 30 days — message us if you need help.

Does one payment cover all my devices?

One payment unlocks one device. Each box or phone you want to use needs its own unlock.

Can I get a refund?

The free trial is there so you can try everything first, so we don't normally refund after you've paid. If AxiomTV doesn't work as described on your device, message us and we'll put it right or refund you. See the refund policy.

How do I get help?

Message us on Discord. Have your device code ready — it's in Settings → Licence, or on the payment screen.

Need help or want to report a problem? Join us on Discord and include your device code.
